Which languages does the translator support?

More than 100. The tool page lists every available language pair.

Can I use DOITNOW on my phone?

Yes. Every page works on smartphones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Is the AI always correct?

No AI is. Review summaries, translations and extracted text before relying on them.
