DOITNOW – Google AdSense activation checklist

1. Publish the website on its final domain and make sure it works on mobile and desktop.
2. Publish complete, accurate About, Contact, Privacy Policy and Terms pages.
3. Add useful original content and test every link and tool.
4. Apply at https://www.google.com/adsense/start/ and submit the website for review.
5. If approved, copy the exact AdSense publisher code and ad-unit slot IDs from your AdSense account.
6. Add the publisher script in the HTML head and place responsive ad units in the marked ad positions.
7. Publish the ads.txt record exactly as supplied by AdSense.

Do not use placeholder publisher IDs or copied ad codes. AdSense approval, ad serving and earnings are controlled by Google and are not guaranteed.
